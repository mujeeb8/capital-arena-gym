Good to know

Frequently
asked questions.

How can I join?

Visit the facility to sign up in person, or contact the club by phone or email to learn about membership options and get started.

What amenities does Capital Arena offer?

The club lists a fitness center, swimming pool, spa and massage services, steam and sauna rooms, a protein bar and locker rooms.

Do you offer personal training sessions?

Yes. The club offers personalized sessions with trainers who tailor workouts to your fitness level and goals.

Are group fitness classes available?

Yes. Published classes include yoga, Pilates, spinning, HIIT and more, led by instructors and catering to different fitness levels.

What are your operating hours?

The listed hours are Monday to Sunday, 9:00 AM–11:00 PM. Contact the club to confirm current hours for each facility.
